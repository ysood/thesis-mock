# Corvel Records Access Act

> Fictional instrument for controlled experiments. Not real law.

## [CRAA 1] Section 1 - Short title

This Act may be cited as the Corvel Records Access Act.

## [CRAA 2] Section 2 - Definitions

In this Act:
'data host' has the meaning given in section 2 of the Health Records Act;
'computing provider' means a person that provides storage, hosting or computing services in Corvel, or that is incorporated in Corvel and provides such services elsewhere;
'Commissioner' means the Records Access Commissioner.

## [CRAA 3] Section 3 - Production orders

1. The Commissioner may, for the purposes of national security or the investigation of serious crime, order a computing provider to produce records that are stored by it or accessible to it, whether in Corvel or elsewhere.
2. An order under subsection (1) does not require the authorisation of a court, and may be made regardless of the nationality or residence of the persons to whom the records relate.

## [CRAA 4] Section 4 - Non-disclosure

An order under section 3 may prohibit the computing provider from informing its customer or any other person of the order, for a period the Commissioner specifies, which may be renewed without limit.

## [CRAA 5] Section 5 - Encrypted records

A computing provider is not required by an order under section 3 to decrypt records, or to produce keys, that it does not hold and cannot obtain.

## [CRAA 6] Section 6 - Review

A person to whom records relate has no right to be notified of, or to seek review of, an order under section 3.
