# Corvel Health Records Act

> Fictional instrument for controlled experiments. Not real law.

## [CHRA 1] Section 1 - Short title

This Act may be cited as the Corvel Health Records Act.

## [CHRA 2] Section 2 - Definitions

In this Act:
'health record' means information, in any form, that relates to the physical or mental health of an individual or to the provision of or payment for health care, and identifies the individual;
'covered provider' means (a) a health care provider that bills a health insurer licensed in Corvel by electronic means, or (b) a health insurer licensed in Corvel;
'service agent' means a person who, on behalf of a covered provider, creates, receives, maintains or transmits health records in order to perform a function for the covered provider;
'data host' means a person that stores health records in Corvel on behalf of another person, whether or not that other person is a covered provider.

## [CHRA 3] Section 3 - Application

1. Sections 4 to 8 apply to covered providers and service agents.
2. A person is not a service agent in respect of health records it maintains for a person that is not a covered provider.
3. Section 9 applies to every data host, whether or not sections 4 to 8 apply to it.

## [CHRA 4] Section 4 - Permitted uses and disclosures

1. A covered provider may use or disclose a health record without the individual's written authorisation only for treatment, payment for health care, or its own health care operations, or as required by law.
2. Any other use or disclosure requires the written authorisation of the individual.

## [CHRA 5] Section 5 - Service agent agreements

A covered provider may disclose health records to a service agent only under a written agreement by which the service agent undertakes to use the records only for the functions performed for the covered provider, to safeguard them, to report any unauthorised use, and to return or destroy them at the end of the agreement where feasible.

## [CHRA 6] Section 6 - Minimum necessary

A covered provider or service agent shall limit any use or disclosure of health records to the minimum necessary to accomplish its purpose, except for disclosures to another health care provider for treatment.

## [CHRA 7] Section 7 - Security

Covered providers and service agents shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of health records.

## [CHRA 8] Section 8 - Breach notification

A covered provider shall notify affected individuals of a breach of unsecured health records within 60 days of discovering it.

## [CHRA 9] Section 9 - Retention by data hosts

1. A data host shall retain every health record it stores for seven years after the last entry in the record, and shall not delete it before that time.
2. Subsection (1) does not apply to a health record that originated outside Corvel where, at the end of its service, the data host returns the record to the person on whose behalf it was stored and retains no copy.
