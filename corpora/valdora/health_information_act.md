# Valdora Health Information Act

> Fictional instrument for controlled experiments. Not real law.

## [VHIA 1] Section 1 - Short title and purpose

1. This Act may be cited as the Valdora Health Information Act.
2. This Act makes provision in the law of Valdora where the Aster Personal Information Regulation so permits, in particular under Article 7(2), Article 8(2)(c), Article 8(3), Article 12(3) and Article 21 of that Regulation.

## [VHIA 2] Section 2 - Interpretation

In this Act:
'registered practitioner' means a person registered as a medical practitioner, nurse or pharmacist with the Valdora Health Professions Board;
'health information', 'genetic information', 'custodian', 'agent' and 'outward transfer' have the meanings given in Article 4 of the Aster Regulation;
'Commission' means the Valdora Information Commission.

## [VHIA 3] Section 3 - Authority

The Valdora Information Commission is designated as the Authority for Valdora for the purposes of Article 21 of the Aster Regulation.

## [VHIA 4] Section 4 - Processing for health care

1. For the purposes of Article 8(2)(c) of the Aster Regulation, the processing of health information is lawful where it is necessary for the provision of health care or treatment and is carried out by or under the responsibility of a registered practitioner.
2. Processing by an agent acting only on the documented instructions of a custodian whose health care is provided by registered practitioners is processing under the responsibility of those practitioners.

## [VHIA 5] Section 5 - Professional secrecy

1. A registered practitioner owes a duty of professional secrecy in respect of health information obtained in the course of practice.
2. A registered practitioner may disclose such information to a third party only with the consent of the patient, where required by law, or where necessary to prevent a serious and imminent threat to life.
3. Making information available to an agent within the meaning of section 4(2) is not a disclosure to a third party for the purposes of subsection (2).

## [VHIA 6] Section 6 - Further conditions for outward transfers of health records

1. Under Article 8(3) of the Aster Regulation, a custodian established in Valdora shall not make an outward transfer of the health records of patients for the purpose of hosting or storing them unless:
(a) the custodian has notified the Commission in writing at least 30 days before the first such transfer; and
(b) the records are encrypted while stored outside the Union, and the keys needed to decrypt them are held only within the Union.
2. Subsection (1) is in addition to the requirements of Articles 16 to 19 of the Aster Regulation.

## [VHIA 7] Section 7 - Genetic information

Genetic information may not be made the subject of an outward transfer on the basis of Article 19(1)(a) of the Aster Regulation, and the prohibition in Article 8(1) of that Regulation may not be lifted by consent in respect of genetic information processed for insurance or employment purposes.

## [VHIA 8] Section 8 - Impact assessment list

Under Article 12(3) of the Aster Regulation, an impact assessment is required in Valdora, regardless of the number of persons concerned, for:
(a) the hosting or storage of a patient record system by an agent outside Valdora;
(b) the matching of health information with information held by an insurer.

## [VHIA 9] Section 9 - Age of consent

For the purposes of Article 7(2) of the Aster Regulation, the age at which a person may consent on their own behalf is 14.
