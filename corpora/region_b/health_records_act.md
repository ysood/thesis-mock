# Region B Health Records Act

> Fictional instrument for controlled experiments. Not real law.

## [B-HRA 1] Section 1 - Short title

This Act may be cited as the Region B Health Records Act.

## [B-HRA 2] Section 2 - Definitions

In this Act:
'health record' means information, in any form, that relates to the physical or mental health of an individual or to the provision of or payment for health care, and identifies the individual;
'covered provider' means (a) a health care provider that bills a health insurer licensed in Region B by electronic means, or (b) a health insurer licensed in Region B;
'service agent' means a person who, on behalf of a covered provider, creates, receives, maintains or transmits health records in order to perform a function for the covered provider;
'data host' means a person that stores health records in Region B on behalf of another person, whether or not that other person is a covered provider.

## [B-HRA 3] Section 3 - Application

1. Sections 4 to 8 apply to covered providers and service agents.
2. A person is not a service agent in respect of health records it maintains for a person that is not a covered provider.
3. Section 9 applies to every data host, whether or not sections 4 to 8 apply to it.

## [B-HRA 4] Section 4 - Permitted uses and disclosures

1. A covered provider may use or disclose a health record without the individual's written authorisation only for treatment, payment for health care, or its own health care operations, or as required by law.
2. Any other use or disclosure requires the written authorisation of the individual.

## [B-HRA 5] Section 5 - Service agent agreements

A covered provider may disclose health records to a service agent only under a written agreement by which the service agent undertakes to use the records only for the functions performed for the covered provider, to safeguard them, to report any unauthorised use, and to return or destroy them at the end of the agreement where feasible.

## [B-HRA 6] Section 6 - Minimum necessary

A covered provider or service agent shall limit any use or disclosure of health records to the minimum necessary to accomplish its purpose, except for disclosures to another health care provider for treatment.

## [B-HRA 7] Section 7 - Security

Covered providers and service agents shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of health records.

## [B-HRA 8] Section 8 - Breach notification

A covered provider shall notify affected individuals of a breach of unsecured health records within 60 days of discovering it.

## [B-HRA 9] Section 9 - Retention by data hosts

1. A data host shall retain every health record it stores for seven years after the last entry in the record, and shall not delete it before that time.
2. Subsection (1) does not apply to a health record that originated outside Region B where, at the end of its service, the data host returns the record to the person on whose behalf it was stored and retains no copy.

## [B-HRA 10] Section 10 - Access by individuals

1. An individual has a right to inspect and obtain a copy of the health records about the individual that a covered provider maintains.
2. A covered provider shall act on a request under subsection (1) within 30 days of receiving it, and may charge only a reasonable cost-based fee.

## [B-HRA 11] Section 11 - Complaints

1. A person who believes that a covered provider or service agent is not complying with sections 4 to 8 may file a complaint with the Health Records Commissioner.
2. The Commissioner shall investigate any complaint that indicates a possible violation due to wilful neglect.

## [B-HRA 12] Section 12 - Civil penalties

1. The Health Records Commissioner may impose a civil penalty on a covered provider, service agent or data host that violates sections 4 to 9.
2. A penalty shall not exceed 50 000 units for each violation, or 1 500 000 units for all violations of an identical provision in a calendar year.
3. No penalty may be imposed for a violation that was not due to wilful neglect and was corrected within 30 days of the person becoming aware of it.

## [B-HRA 13] Section 13 - Regulations

The Health Records Commissioner may make regulations prescribing the form of written authorisations, the content of service agent agreements and the safeguards required by this Act.
