# Region A Personal Information Regulation

> Fictional instrument for controlled experiments. Not real law.

## [A-REG 1] Article 1 - Subject matter

1. This Regulation lays down rules on the processing of personal information and on its movement within and outside Region A.
2. Subregions may adopt provisions of their own law only where this Regulation expressly permits them to do so.

## [A-REG 2] Article 2 - Material scope

1. This Regulation applies to the processing of personal information wholly or partly by automated means, and to other processing of personal information which forms part of a filing system.
2. This Regulation does not apply to processing:
(a) by a natural person in the course of a purely personal or household activity;
(b) of information which does not relate to an identified or identifiable person, including information rendered anonymous in such a manner that no person can be identified by any means reasonably likely to be used by the custodian or by any other person.

## [A-REG 3] Article 3 - Territorial scope

1. This Regulation applies to processing in the context of the activities of an establishment of a custodian or an agent in a Subregion, regardless of whether the processing itself takes place in Region A.
2. This Regulation also applies to processing by a custodian or agent not established in Region A where the processing relates to offering goods or services to persons in Region A or to monitoring their behaviour within Region A.

## [A-REG 4] Article 4 - Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation:
(1) 'personal information' means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person;
(2) 'health information' means personal information about the physical or mental health of a person, including information revealed by the provision of health care to that person;
(3) 'genetic information' means personal information relating to the inherited or acquired genetic characteristics of a person;
(4) 'sensitive information' means health information, genetic information and biometric information processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying a person;
(5) 'custodian' means the person or body which, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing;
(6) 'agent' means a person or body which processes personal information on behalf of a custodian;
(7) 'pseudonymised information' means personal information which can no longer be attributed to a specific person without the use of additional information kept separately under technical and organisational measures;
(8) 'third territory' means any state or territory which is not a Subregion;
(9) 'outward transfer' means making personal information available to a recipient in a third territory, whether by sending, storing or hosting it there, or by permitting it to be accessed from a third territory;
(10) 'Subregion' means a subregion of Region A listed in Annex I;
(11) 'Authority' means the supervisory authority designated by a Subregion under Article 21.

## [A-REG 5] Article 5 - Principles

1. Personal information shall be:
(a) processed lawfully, fairly and transparently;
(b) collected for specified and explicit purposes and not further processed in a manner incompatible with them;
(c) adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for those purposes; where a purpose can be achieved with pseudonymised information, identifiable information shall not be used for it;
(d) accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date;
(e) kept in identifiable form for no longer than is necessary for the purposes;
(f) protected by appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
2. The custodian shall be responsible for, and able to demonstrate, compliance with paragraph 1.

## [A-REG 6] Article 6 - Lawful grounds

1. Processing is lawful only if and to the extent that at least one of the following grounds applies:
(a) the person has consented to the processing for one or more specific purposes in accordance with Article 7;
(b) the processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the person is a party, or for steps taken at the person's request before entering into a contract;
(c) the processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation under Region A or Subregion law to which the custodian is subject;
(d) the processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the person or of another natural person;
(e) the processing is necessary for the performance of a public function vested in the custodian by Region A or Subregion law;
(f) the processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of the custodian or a third party, except where those interests are overridden by the interests or rights of the person.
2. Point (f) of paragraph 1 is not available to public bodies in the performance of their functions, and is not available for the processing of sensitive information.

## [A-REG 7] Article 7 - Conditions for consent

1. Consent shall be given by a clear written or electronic statement, specific to each purpose, and may be withdrawn at any time as easily as it was given.
2. A person under the age of 16 may not consent on their own behalf; consent shall be given by a person with parental responsibility. Subregion law may set a lower age, which shall not be below 13.

## [A-REG 8] Article 8 - Sensitive information

1. Processing of sensitive information is prohibited.
2. Paragraph 1 does not apply where:
(a) the person has given explicit consent to the processing for one or more specified purposes, except where Subregion law provides that the prohibition may not be lifted by consent;
(b) the processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the person or of another natural person where the person is physically or legally incapable of giving consent;
(c) the processing is necessary for the purposes of preventive medicine, medical diagnosis or the provision of health care or treatment, on the basis of Subregion law, and the information is processed by or under the responsibility of a professional subject to an obligation of professional secrecy under Subregion law;
(d) the processing is necessary for reasons of public interest in the area of public health on the basis of Region A or Subregion law;
(e) the processing is necessary for scientific research in accordance with Article 20.
3. Subregions may maintain or introduce further conditions, including limitations, with regard to the processing of health information or genetic information.

## [A-REG 9] Article 9 - Agents

1. A custodian shall use only agents providing sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures so that the processing meets the requirements of this Regulation.
2. Processing by an agent shall be governed by a contract that is binding on the agent with regard to the custodian and sets out the subject-matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of information and the categories of persons. The contract shall require the agent to:
(a) process the information only on documented instructions from the custodian, including with regard to outward transfers;
(b) ensure that persons authorised to process the information are bound by confidentiality;
(c) take the measures required by Article 14;
(d) not engage another agent without the prior written authorisation of the custodian;
(e) assist the custodian in meeting its obligations under this Regulation;
(f) at the choice of the custodian, delete or return all the information at the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless Region A or Subregion law requires their storage;
(g) make available to the custodian all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Article.
3. An agent which determines the purposes and means of processing shall be considered a custodian in respect of that processing.

## [A-REG 10] Article 10 - Joint custodians

Where two or more custodians jointly determine the purposes and means of processing, they shall determine their respective responsibilities by an arrangement between them, the essence of which shall be made available to the persons concerned.

## [A-REG 11] Article 11 - Information to be given to persons

1. When collecting personal information, the custodian shall inform the person of the custodian's identity, the purposes and lawful ground of the processing, the recipients or categories of recipients, the storage period and the person's rights.
2. Where the custodian intends to make an outward transfer, it shall also inform the person of that intention, of the third territory concerned and of the route under Articles 17 to 19 relied on.

## [A-REG 12] Article 12 - Impact assessment

1. Where processing is likely to result in a high risk to the rights of persons, the custodian shall, before the processing, carry out an assessment of the impact of the envisaged processing.
2. An impact assessment is required in particular in the case of:
(a) processing of the sensitive information of more than 5,000 persons;
(b) systematic monitoring of a publicly accessible area on a large scale.
3. Each Authority may establish and publish a list of further kinds of processing for which an impact assessment is required in its Subregion.
4. The assessment shall contain at least a description of the processing, an assessment of its necessity, an assessment of the risks and the measures envisaged to address them.

## [A-REG 13] Article 13 - Records of processing

Each custodian and agent shall maintain a written record of the processing activities under its responsibility, including the purposes, the categories of information and persons, the recipients, any outward transfers and the route relied on, and the storage periods.

## [A-REG 14] Article 14 - Security

The custodian and the agent shall implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including as appropriate encryption, the ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality and availability, and a process for regularly testing the effectiveness of those measures.

## [A-REG 15] Article 15 - Notification of a security breach

A custodian shall notify the competent Authority of a breach of security affecting personal information within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to persons. An agent shall notify the custodian without undue delay.

## [A-REG 16] Article 16 - General principle for outward transfers

An outward transfer may take place only where the other provisions of this Regulation are complied with and the transfer is covered by Article 17, Article 18 or Article 19.

## [A-REG 17] Article 17 - Recognised third territories

1. The Commission may recognise that a third territory, a specified sector within it, or recipients holding a specified certification in it, ensure an adequate level of protection.
2. Recognitions in force are listed in Annex II, together with any limits attached to them.
3. A transfer is covered by a recognition only within its stated limits. A recognition limited to certified recipients covers only a recipient whose certification is active at the time of the transfer and extends to the kind of information transferred.

## [A-REG 18] Article 18 - Approved transfer clauses

1. In the absence of a recognition covering the transfer, an outward transfer may take place where the exporter and the recipient have executed the transfer clauses approved by the Commission.
2. Before relying on paragraph 1, the exporter shall assess whether the law and practice of the third territory applicable to the recipient, including any power of public authorities to require disclosure of or access to the information, would prevent the recipient from complying with the clauses, and shall adopt supplementary measures where necessary to ensure protection essentially equivalent to that within Region A.
3. Where effective supplementary measures cannot be adopted, the transfer shall not take place on the basis of this Article.

## [A-REG 19] Article 19 - Derogations for specific situations

1. In the absence of a recognition under Article 17 or of approved transfer clauses under Article 18, an outward transfer may take place only on one of the following conditions:
(a) the person has explicitly consented to the proposed transfer after having been informed of the possible risks of the transfer in the absence of a recognition and of appropriate safeguards;
(b) the transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract between the person and the custodian;
(c) the transfer is necessary to protect the vital interests of the person or of other persons, where the person is physically or legally incapable of giving consent;
(d) the transfer is necessary for important reasons of public interest recognised in Region A law or in the law of the Subregion to which the custodian is subject.
2. Points (a) and (b) of paragraph 1 apply only to transfers which are occasional and do not form part of a repetitive, structural or continuing series of transfers.
3. Points (a) and (b) of paragraph 1 are not available to public bodies in the performance of their functions.

## [A-REG 20] Article 20 - Scientific research

Processing for scientific research shall be subject to appropriate safeguards, including pseudonymisation where the purpose can be fulfilled in that way. Subregion law may provide for derogations from the rights of persons in so far as they would render the research impossible.

## [A-REG 21] Article 21 - Subregion law and Authorities

1. Each Subregion shall designate one or more Authorities to monitor the application of this Regulation in its territory.
2. Subregion law may make provision where this Regulation so permits, including under Article 7(2), Article 8(2)(a) and (c), Article 8(3), Article 12(3), Article 19(1)(d) and Article 20.
3. Where a custodian is established in a Subregion, the law of that Subregion adopted under paragraph 2 applies to its processing in addition to this Regulation.

## [A-REG 22] Article 22 - Independence of Authorities

1. Each Authority shall act with complete independence in performing its tasks and exercising its powers under this Regulation.
2. The members of each Authority shall remain free from external influence and shall neither seek nor take instructions from anybody.
3. Each Subregion shall ensure that its Authority is provided with the staff, premises and financial resources necessary for the effective performance of its tasks.

## [A-REG 23] Article 23 - Tasks of Authorities

1. Each Authority shall in its territory:
(a) monitor and enforce the application of this Regulation;
(b) promote public awareness of the risks, rules and rights in relation to processing;
(c) handle complaints lodged under Article 26 and inform the complainant of the progress and outcome within a reasonable period;
(d) cooperate with other Authorities with a view to ensuring the consistent application of this Regulation;
(e) conduct investigations on the application of this Regulation.
2. The performance of the tasks of each Authority shall be free of charge for the person concerned.

## [A-REG 24] Article 24 - Powers of Authorities

1. Each Authority shall have the power to order a custodian or agent to provide any information it requires for the performance of its tasks, to carry out audits, and to obtain access to any premises.
2. Each Authority shall have the power to issue warnings and reprimands, to order that processing be brought into compliance, to impose a temporary or definitive limitation on processing, and to impose an administrative fine under Article 29.
3. The exercise of these powers shall be subject to appropriate safeguards, including an effective judicial remedy and due process.

## [A-REG 25] Article 25 - Region A Information Board

1. A Region A Information Board is established, composed of the head of each Authority.
2. The Board shall issue guidelines, recommendations and best practices to encourage the consistent application of this Regulation, and shall advise the Commission on any proposed amendment of this Regulation.
3. The Board shall draw up an annual report, which shall be made public.

## [A-REG 26] Article 26 - Right to lodge a complaint

Every person who considers that the processing of personal information relating to them infringes this Regulation shall have the right to lodge a complaint with an Authority, in particular in the Subregion of their habitual residence or place of work.

## [A-REG 27] Article 27 - Right to an effective judicial remedy

1. Each natural or legal person shall have the right to an effective judicial remedy against a legally binding decision of an Authority concerning them.
2. Each person shall have the right to an effective judicial remedy against a custodian or agent where they consider that their rights under this Regulation have been infringed as a result of processing in breach of this Regulation.

## [A-REG 28] Article 28 - Right to compensation and liability

1. Any person who has suffered material or non-material damage as a result of an infringement of this Regulation shall have the right to receive compensation from the custodian or agent for the damage suffered.
2. An agent shall be liable for the damage caused by processing only where it has not complied with the obligations of this Regulation specifically directed to agents or where it has acted outside or contrary to the lawful instructions of the custodian.

## [A-REG 29] Article 29 - Administrative fines

1. Each Authority shall ensure that administrative fines imposed under this Article are effective, proportionate and dissuasive in each individual case.
2. Infringements of Article 9 and Articles 12 to 15 shall be subject to administrative fines of up to 10 000 000 units or, in the case of an undertaking, up to 2% of its total annual turnover, whichever is higher.
3. Infringements of Articles 5 to 8, Article 11 and Articles 16 to 19 shall be subject to administrative fines of up to 20 000 000 units or, in the case of an undertaking, up to 4% of its total annual turnover, whichever is higher.
4. When deciding whether to impose a fine and on its amount, due regard shall be given to the nature, gravity and duration of the infringement, whether it was intentional or negligent, and any action taken to mitigate the damage.

## [A-REG 30] Article 30 - Processing and freedom of expression

Subregion law shall reconcile the right to the protection of personal information under this Regulation with the right to freedom of expression and information, including processing for journalistic purposes and for the purposes of academic, artistic or literary expression.

## [A-REG 31] Article 31 - Processing in the context of employment

Subregion law may provide for more specific rules to ensure the protection of the personal information of employees in the employment context, in particular for the purposes of recruitment, the performance of the contract of employment and the management of work.

## [A-REG 32] Article 32 - Entry into force and application

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication and shall apply from the first day of the second year following its entry into force.
