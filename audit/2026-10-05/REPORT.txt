LEGAL KNOWLEDGE GRAPH AUDIT, 5 OCTOBER 2026

Decision: DO NOT FREEZE YET.

The audit found substantive modelling errors, not just missing citations. They have
been repaired where the present data model permits a focused correction. The revised
graph is better supported and more conservative, but cannot yet justify a claim that
every legal conclusion is correct. The main remaining issue is independent answers
for each actor and processing operation. Coverage and operative-law limits also remain.

What was examined

The authored normative graph in reference/knowledge_graph.json, its construction and
three-valued evaluator in app/kg.py, the request-to-node bindings, its source bundles,
and national-law implementation links. Every final edge is inventoried. The 20
requirements have individual review notes in review.json. The pre-audit graph is
retained in graph_before.json so the change can be inspected independently of Git.
This is a bounded GDPR/HIPAA/selected-Irish-law audit, not all Irish or US law.

Evidence and verification

- Final graph: 276 nodes and 471 edges, including 52 routes and 67 conditions.
- 303 quoted passages (including opening and assessment-unit metadata) matched
  retained official publisher texts. No quote mismatch or snapshot hash error.
- Graph structural validation returned no issues.
- 213 offline tests passed, including 14 new legal counterexample tests.
- No paid model assessments were run. These tests do not establish the correctness
  of LLM answers on all 15 scenarios. Many test facts are synthetic inputs to the
  evaluator, not legally validated case findings.

Run from prototype:
  .venv/bin/python audit/2026-10-05/check_sources.py
  .venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests -q

verification.json pins the graph SHA-256 and records the exact counts and source
coverage. provenance_checks.json records every quote check. sources.json records
publisher URLs, retrieval dates and SHA-256 hashes. The check exits unsuccessfully
if graph validation, snapshot hashes or quote matching fail.

The edges do not all make the same kind of claim:
- 291 have a statutory quotation. A quotation attached to a route enumeration is
  evidence of its source, not proof that every interpretation of the edge is sound.
- 110 are authored legal dependencies, such as a necessary condition or scope.
- 65 are intake-policy links from request fields to legal predicates.
- 5 are non-inferential comparisons between concepts across regimes.

The last three categories must not be presented as quotations from legislation.
Even an authored dependency sharing the same article with another node needs a
substantive interpretation. edge_ledger.json links each edge to its relevant
requirement review; it deliberately does not stamp every edge 'legally certified'.

Principal repairs

1. Purpose labels and 'in place' arrangement fields no longer prove a complete
   legal test. GDPR roles, biometrics and the EEA geography flag are also partial
   hints. Negative inference from filled fields needs an explicitly exhaustive
   mapping. A US-to-US disclosure can still require a Chapter V assessment if the
   exporter is subject to GDPR for that processing; geography alone cannot deny it.

2. Every route now has a qualified core statement. These include necessity and
   balancing, valid consent, Article 49 subsidiarity and restrictions, and the
   detailed HIPAA and Irish conditions that short labels omitted. These statements
   are still bundled legal tests that the specialist must assess in full.

3. HIPAA covered-entity permissions are scoped to that capacity. Business associates
   require separate authority under the applicable agreement and law. Recipient
   safeguards distinguish the two capacities, and business-associate relationships
   are specific to the sender's processing. A BAA is not a universal permission.

4. All six minimum-necessary exception categories are represented independently of
   covered-entity route scope. The Security Rule is scoped to electronic PHI.
   The limited-data-set route cannot drop its own DUA prerequisite by denying that
   it relies on the limited-data-set permission.

5. Article 35(10) is an explicit DPIA exception. The Article 9(2)(a) proviso is an
   explicit exception to the consent route. A single practitioner is not assumed
   exempt from every possible DPIA trigger.

6. Irish DPA 2018 sections 54 and 42 supply the selected research legal basis.
   The Health Research Regulations are additional safeguards, including when
   Article 9 explicit consent is selected. Necessity in sections 52 and 53 is
   explicit. Failure of one selected national basis does not rule out all other
   law or the Article 9(2)(h) contract alternative.

7. Unmodelled placeholders cannot establish permission. An open list whose known
   routes all fail remains unresolved. The HIPAA permission list and Irish research
   alternatives are explicitly open rather than overstating completeness.

8. A dated HHS operative-law notice now accompanies affected HIPAA source text.
   HHS reports that most of the 2024 reproductive-health rule was vacated on
   18 June 2025. Printed regulatory text alone cannot establish its operation.
   This notice is a mitigation, not a machine-enforced legal-status filter.

Official evidence and limits

GDPR: EU Publications Office article and recital snapshots, with EUR-Lex links for
individual provisions. Matching is within the relevant article or recital.

HIPAA: official GovInfo 2025 annual CFR XML, parts 160 and 164, used for reproducible
section-level matching. Current eCFR was inspected separately through the web reader
and advertised currency to 1 October 2026. Direct downloads returned access challenges,
which are explicitly marked and excluded from evidence matching. The annual snapshot
is not represented as a complete current-law audit. The HHS notice is separately
recorded in reference/legal_status.json and web_review.json.

Ireland: Law Reform Commission revised DPA 2018, advertised as updated 16 July 2026
with changes known in force through 17 September 2026; Irish Statute Book official
SI 314/2018, SI 188/2019 and SI 18/2021, plus the amendment index. The original and
amending instruments were read together. Automated SI matching is instrument-level,
so it cannot independently validate each pinpoint or amendment's operation.

Publisher matching removes spacing and punctuation while preserving word/number
order. This tolerates HTML formatting, but is weaker than a proof of precise legal
entailment. The ordinary graph validator also checks quotations against the local
corpus. The runtime law files were not overwritten.

Guidance and case-law observations are listed in web_review.json with distinct
retrieval statuses. Not every cited guidance document received a new full-document
review. The EDPB supplementary-measures PDF was rate-limited; its official landing
page was checked. Scholarly ontology papers are methodological references, not
statutory authority, and were not revalidated as part of this legal-source audit.

Before a freeze

A. Actor and operation identity: today, one node has one answer for the whole case.
   Sender disclosure, recipient receipt and later reuse may have different scope,
   legal bases and conditions. A universal-sounding statement does not fix that
   representation. Either implement independent assessment units or enforce and
   test a narrower supported-case contract.

B. Coverage: Irish health research using ordinary personal data must reliably reach
   national safeguards without depending on the special-category question. HIPAA
   special scope exclusions and unmodelled routes need defined handling. US state
   law and other federal/national regimes are outside this bounded graph. Unsupported
   cases must remain identifiable rather than silently receiving full assurance.

C. Legal judgment: source and fact-pointer checks do not prove that the cited facts
   satisfy the complete legal test. Review expected condition answers and outcomes
   for the 15 scenarios and adversarial variants with legal expertise. No independent
   legal sign-off has been obtained by this audit.

D. Time: choose an explicit as-of date and retain operative versions, amendments,
   applicable court decisions and material interpretive guidance. Add a way to
   invalidate or re-review a frozen version when those sources change. Freezing
   software cannot freeze the law.

E. Policy: advisory checklist classes, standing-activity treatment, 'not claimed
   as described' route closure and the trust placed in supplied classifications
   are system policies. The statutory quotations do not enact those choices.

Handoff

Changed runtime files: app/kg.py, app/knowledge.py, app/agents.py,
reference/knowledge_graph.json and reference/legal_status.json.
Updated tests and existing architecture documentation; added the dated audit and
counterexamples. Existing user changes in app/llm.py, ui.py and the untracked scenario
and law files were not edited. No commit or push was made.

Neo4j remains closed. Its retained sandbox is the PRE-AUDIT snapshot, not the revised
live graph. Do not use that visualisation as evidence of these repaired semantics.
